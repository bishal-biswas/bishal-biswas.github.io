**Last updated: 27 August 2026**

### The short version

**Some links on this website are affiliate links.** If you click one and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission - **at no additional cost to you**. You never pay more because you used our link.

### What is an affiliate link?

An affiliate link is a special tracking link. When you click it and buy something, the retailer records that the visit came from this website and pays us a small percentage of the sale.

The price you pay is exactly the same as it would be if you had gone to the retailer directly. The commission comes out of the retailer's margin, not your pocket.

### Where these links appear

Affiliate links may appear:

- on the **Products** page, where we list tools, books, courses, and hardware we recommend;
- within articles and tutorials, when a product or service is mentioned; and
- occasionally in other site content.

- A commission **never** determines whether a product is recommended, how it is rated, or what we say about it.

- Opinions expressed are our own and are not directed, reviewed, or approved by any advertiser or affiliate partner.
- We will not recommend something we would not suggest to a friend.
